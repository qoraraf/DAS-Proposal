Initiative 1

FREEWHEEL | دحديرة

Proposed by
Sedra Abdul Ghaffar A. Basir
Age group
Grades 6–9, approximately 11–15 years
Number of sessions
Not specified
Material category
Woodworking Cardboard & foam prototyping 3D printing Laser cutting Electronics Digital media

A hands-on after-school adventure where students design, build, brand, and race their own gravity-powered cars. The program combines woodworking, 3D design, electronics, filmmaking, sponsorship, and Saudi-inspired branding. It ends with the Freewheel Festival, where teams reveal their cars, tell their stories, and race for glory.

Environmental Art

Proposed by
Osama
Age group
10–18 years
Number of sessions
36 sessions over 12 weeks, 3 days per week
Material category
Recycled materials Upcycled materials Art & craft Cardboard & plastics Paint & adhesives

“Our Ocean Is Crying” is an environmental art project where students create an aquarium-style installation from recycled and upcycled materials. The initiative combines sustainability, creativity, and hands-on making. Students transform waste materials into a meaningful artistic exhibition.

Initiative 6

Proposed by
Asmaa Sinan
Age group
10–14 years
Number of sessions
Not specified
Material category
Paper Cardstock Folding & scoring tools Glue & tape Cutting mats

This initiative explores how flat paper can become moving 3D structures through folding, geometry, and repeated patterns.
