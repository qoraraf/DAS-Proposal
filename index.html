03 · Gap Analysis

The missing “Maker Layer” — where we add unique value

Already in the current offer

  • Exam-prep classes (Tahseeli, Qudurat, IB, AP)
  • Team-sport training & tournaments
  • Traditional art studios & calligraphy
  • Language clubs & leadership talks
  • Screen-based digital awareness (AI, Canva)

Not offered — the Maker Layer

  • Digital fabrication — 3D printing, laser cutting & engraving
  • Physical computing — Arduino, NodeMCU, Crumble, Makey Makey
  • Engineering design process & rapid prototyping
  • E-textiles & soft circuits — crochet meets conductivity
  • Sustainability engineering projects with real-world impact
The proposed list prepares students for exams and tournaments. Our makerspace prepares them to build the future — with their own hands.

06 · Delivery Model

A turnkey, safety-first delivery model

≤ 15 Makers per Cohort

The gold-standard safety ratio for laser, wood & 3D-print machinery — and true mentorship.

4-Week Sprint Cadence

1–2 sessions per week, 90 minutes — 4 to 8 focused contact hours ending in a shipped product.

Safety First

Student machine “licenses”, PPE, supervised stations and certified instructors at every bench.

Gender-Separated, Skill-Equal

Identical core curriculum & certification for boys’ and girls’ cohorts; project themes personalized.

Visible ROI

Monthly Maker Showcase for parents, take-home products, digital portfolios & certificates.

Turnkey for DAS

We bring equipment, consumables, curriculum and assessment — DAS provides the room.
